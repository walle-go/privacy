Walle Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Walle browser extension processes data when it is used by authorized internal users for recruiting, candidate management, duplicate-checking, workflow assistance, and related productivity tasks.

Effective date: 2026-11-23 Publisher: Walle Contact: wallego71@gmail.com
01

Scope, legal effect, and single purpose

This Privacy Policy describes the categories of data that the Walle browser extension may process, the purposes for which that data may be processed, and the operational restrictions that apply when the extension is used by authorized internal users. It is intended to function as a privacy disclosure, compliance-control statement, and operational boundary document for the extension.

Walle is an internal productivity extension intended only for authorized employees, contractors, or other personnel acting under an approved business account or company workflow. Its single purpose is to support work-related recruiting, candidate-management, CRM, duplicate-checking, workflow-assistance, status-reporting, file-handling, and related operational record activities.

This Policy does not grant any license, ownership right, collection right, scraping right, employment right, platform-access right, or other independent legal entitlement to any user. It also does not override third-party platform terms, employer policies, candidate notices, contractual restrictions, confidentiality duties, data-protection obligations, or applicable law.

Walle is not a public web-crawling, scraping, resale, advertising, surveillance, credit-scoring, consumer-profiling, or data-brokerage service. This Policy applies only to the Walle extension and does not govern the independent privacy practices of third-party websites, recruiting platforms, CRM systems, applicant tracking systems, cloud providers, or other external services.

02

Parties, roles, and authorization model

Walle is published by Walle. The organization, team, customer, or business unit that authorizes use of Walle for a particular workflow is responsible for ensuring that its users have the necessary employment, contractual, platform, candidate, and legal authorization for the relevant activity.

Where privacy laws use terms such as controller, processor, business, service provider, operator, or similar role labels, the applicable role may depend on the relevant workflow, the organization directing the activity, the system of record, and the data source. In general, Walle is designed to act as an operational tool for authorized business users and not as an independent owner of third-party platform content or candidate records.

Users may use Walle only on work platforms, accounts, pages, files, and records that they are authorized to access in the ordinary course of their duties. Walle does not grant any independent right to copy, collect, export, enrich, reuse, disclose, transmit, or redistribute third-party website content, candidate information, CRM records, attachments, resumes, or other protected materials.

  • Walle should not be used to access, collect, or export data where doing so is prohibited by law, contract, platform terms, website notices, access controls, or the user's employer policies.
  • Walle is not designed to bypass login requirements, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, anti-bot controls, rate limits, or other technical restrictions.
  • Walle should not be used for bulk public crawling, resale of website data, advertising profiling, or any purpose unrelated to authorized internal recruiting and CRM work.
  • If a supported website owner, platform operator, candidate, employer, system administrator, or competent authority raises a valid access, privacy, intellectual-property, confidentiality, employment, contractual, or data-protection concern, we will review the concern and may restrict, disable, delete, suspend, or stop processing the relevant data, feature, account, workflow, or site support.

These safeguards reduce risk but do not replace separate legal permission, platform permission, candidate notice or consent, employment authorization, contractual review, security review, data-protection assessment, or professional legal review where required.

03

Information Walle may process

Depending on which feature is used, Walle may process the following categories of data from supported work pages, user-selected records, local browser state, and approved company systems:

  • Supported-page information: current URL, page type, page state, page identifiers, visible workflow context, selected rows or records, navigation status, and signals needed to determine whether a Walle feature is available on the current page.
  • Recruiting and CRM information: candidate names, profile URLs, profile identifiers, job titles, employer or education information, resume or attachment information, job or position details, duplicate-check results, CRM IDs, import results, recommendation or submission status, task status, workflow notes, and similar business records.
  • Contact information: phone numbers, email addresses, public contact URLs, masked contact values, hashed contact values, and other contact fields only when visible to an authorized user, entered by the user, already present in a user-selected work record, or required for an authorized workflow.
  • File and attachment information: file names, download status, resume or attachment metadata, user-requested export files, and related processing state where a feature requires resume handling, attachment download, export preparation, or task recovery.
  • Operational and diagnostic data: task status, quota or usage status, selected workflow options, feature flags, error messages, diagnostic logs, timestamps, extension version, browser state relevant to the feature, and feedback submitted by the user.
  • Account and session information: login status, company-system user identifiers, user names returned by authorized company systems, tenant or workspace indicators, and session cookies or authentication signals already present in the browser for supported work systems. Walle does not ask users to provide passwords to the extension.

Walle does not intentionally request passwords, payment card data, government identification numbers, biometric data, medical data, precise consumer location data, children's data, or other sensitive personal information unless such data is already included by a third party or by the user in a work record that Walle is instructed to process. Users should not use Walle to collect, upload, transmit, or enrich sensitive data unless they have clear authorization, a lawful business need, and any legally required notice or consent.

Incidental processing may occur when sensitive or unrelated information appears inside a page, resume, attachment, note, or business record selected by an authorized user. In that case, Walle's processing remains limited to the user-started feature and the single purpose described in this Policy.

Walle processes data only for the extension's disclosed single purpose and directly related operational purposes.

Depending on the jurisdiction and workflow, the legal or operational basis for a particular activity may include user initiation, employer authorization, performance of an authorized business process, legitimate business interests in recruiting or CRM operations, compliance with legal obligations, security and abuse-prevention needs, or consent where consent is legally required. The organization directing a workflow remains responsible for determining and documenting the applicable legal basis where law requires a specific basis.

  • Walle does not use processed data to serve personalized, re-targeted, behavioral, or interest-based advertising.
  • Walle does not sell, rent, trade, broker, or license processed data as a standalone data product.
  • Walle does not use processed data for consumer credit, lending, insurance eligibility, housing eligibility, or unrelated background-screening determinations.

  • Walle does not intentionally collect unrelated browsing history or build profiles about a user's activity outside the supported work pages and user-started workflows described in this Policy.
05

Supported sites, remote services, and browser permissions

Walle requests browser access only for the business systems and page types needed by its single purpose. These may include company CRM systems, authorized recruiting platforms, applicant tracking systems, professional networking or candidate-management pages, and company workflow integrations.

Site support and host permissions are used only so authorized users can read relevant page context, compare records, prepare workflow data, submit user-requested actions, download user-requested files, and synchronize status with approved business systems. A supported site name or technical permission does not mean Walle owns, endorses, controls, or has independent permission to reuse that site's content. Users remain responsible for using Walle only within their account permissions, employer authorization, applicable platform terms, applicable notices, confidentiality obligations, and applicable law.

Walle's executable extension code is packaged with the extension. Walle does not load or execute JavaScript, WebAssembly, or extension logic from remote servers. Walle may communicate with remote business systems, workflow endpoints, or pages that the authorized user is already using. Those remote interactions exchange data, status, configuration, or diagnostics needed to verify session state, retrieve workflow status, perform duplicate checks, submit authorized records, receive quota or task results, and provide support diagnostics. They are limited to the single purpose described in this Policy and are not used for advertising, unrelated profiling, or hidden feature delivery.

  • host permissions allow Walle to operate only on supported business pages and approved workflow endpoints needed for authorized recruiting and CRM tasks.
  • storage stores preferences, task state, local caches, quota/status data, and settings in the user's browser.
  • tabs identifies supported tab URLs and coordinates background workflows across pages opened by the user. Walle does not use this permission to build unrelated browsing-history profiles.
  • activeTab lets Walle act on the current supported page after a user opens or starts a relevant workflow.
  • scripting runs packaged extension logic on supported pages so the user-requested workflow can read page context, fill fields, or report results.
  • cookies checks login/session state for supported work systems and, when the user starts an authorized workflow, allows required work-system requests through the user's existing authenticated session. Walle does not export cookie values for advertising, tracking, resale, or unrelated browsing analysis.
  • downloads supports user-requested file, resume, attachment, or export downloads.
  • downloads.ui temporarily hides Chrome's download popup in the current browser profile during user-started background attachment batches, then restores it when the batch stops.
  • notifications shows task progress, completion, error, or action-needed messages.
  • alarms supports scheduled status, quota, version, cleanup, or reliability checks.
06

How information is used

Walle uses processed information only to provide, maintain, secure, audit, troubleshoot, or improve internal recruiting and CRM productivity features that are reasonably related to the disclosed single purpose, including:

  • candidate duplicate checks, CRM lookup, and import workflows;
  • candidate review, resume or attachment handling, and supported submission workflows;
  • job, position, quota, and task status display;
  • internal workflow notifications, user feedback, diagnostics, and support;
  • security, abuse prevention, reliability checks, permission validation, and error recovery for the extension;
  • record reconciliation, duplicate-result verification, local task recovery, and operational audit trails required for the authorized workflow.

Walle does not sell personal information, does not use processed data for advertising, does not build public data products, and does not profile individuals for purposes unrelated to the authorized work features described in this Policy.

Any improvement, measurement, diagnostic, or security use must be proportionate to the feature being supported and must not expand Walle beyond the disclosed single purpose.

07

Local storage, cookies, and browser data

Walle may store preferences, workflow state, quota state, local duplicate-check cache entries, and task logs in chrome.storage on the user's device. Local data remains in the browser until it expires, is overwritten, is cleared by the user, or the extension is removed.

Where a workflow requires contact exchange, export preparation, duplicate checking, or task recovery, contact values may be held temporarily in local browser state or in user-requested records. Walle minimizes local contact retention where feasible by masking, hashing, omitting, overwriting, or expiring values that are no longer needed for the active workflow.

Walle may read cookies only for supported work systems where login/session checks or user-started authorized system requests are required. Cookie values are not used to build cross-site browsing profiles, advertising audiences, resale datasets, or consumer tracking records.

Walle does not ask users to type passwords into the extension and does not use cookie access to defeat access controls, impersonate unrelated users, or maintain an independent session outside the user's authenticated browser context. If the user logs out of a supported work system, clears cookies, removes the extension, or loses authorization, related Walle features may stop functioning.

08

Transmission, retention, and security

  • Transmission: Data sent to company-controlled services, CRM systems, supported recruiting systems, or workflow tools is transmitted through HTTPS or the security mechanism provided by the relevant service, except where a local development or internal testing configuration is used by authorized operators before publication.
  • Storage: Server-side records are stored in company-controlled systems or service providers used to operate the extension and related recruiting workflows. Local records may be stored in browser storage on the user's device.
  • Access controls: Access to operational systems should be limited by account permissions, role-based access, least-privilege principles, and administrative controls appropriate to the relevant environment.
  • Logging and auditability: Operational logs may be used to troubleshoot failures, measure reliability, verify quotas, investigate abuse, and support compliance review. Logs should avoid unnecessary contact values or sensitive content where feasible.
  • Security: We use access controls, transport encryption, operational logging, data minimization, masking, hashing, omission, overwrite, and de-identification measures where feasible. No security measure can guarantee absolute security, and users should not submit data that they are not authorized to process.
  • Retention: Operational records created by Walle are retained only as long as needed for the relevant workflow, audit, security, support, dispute, compliance, or lawful business-record purpose, then deleted, expired, aggregated, or de-identified according to applicable company or service-provider policies. Records stored in third-party CRM, recruiting, applicant tracking, file, messaging, or workflow systems are governed by those systems' retention settings, administrator controls, legal holds, backup practices, and legal requirements.
